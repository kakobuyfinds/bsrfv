What to check before ordering with OrientDig

  1. Verify the original listing. Open the CAFANS product page, locate the seller URL and check that OrientDig accepts it. Keep the seller URL, size and color together in your shortlist.
  2. Compare actual measurements. For a jacket, lay a jacket you already own flat and measure the chest width and sleeve length. Check where the seller starts each measurement before comparing numbers.
  3. Review warehouse photos if available. Match the size label, color and identifying details to your chosen variant. Ask about discrepancies while you can still review the seller's return or exchange options.
  4. Compare the total for your destination. Add the item price, domestic delivery, agent charges and international shipping. Check whether the shipping quote uses actual or volumetric weight and whether taxes or insurance are included.

OrientDig spreadsheet FAQ

What is an OrientDig spreadsheet?

It is a product shortlist used to compare marketplace finds before ordering through the OrientDig shopping agent. This edition lists selected CAFANS product pages with reference prices in CNY and USD.

Do the prices include shipping?

The table shows item prices. Check domestic delivery, agent fees, international shipping and any taxes for your specific order before paying.

What if OrientDig does not accept a product link?

Open the CAFANS product page and find the original marketplace URL. Try that URL in OrientDig. If it still fails, ask OrientDig whether it supports that marketplace and listing.
